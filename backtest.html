Does the research grade help?

The grade follows how established evaluators work: CRISIL's IPO grading (business and financial prospects, management, governance, graded 1 to 5 relative to other listed companies and explicitly not a price opinion); Renaissance Capital (fundamentals, governance, valuation against listed peers, insider selling); and Indian broker IPO notes (valuation against peers as the main reason to avoid, growth and return on capital as the reason to pay up). Pillars: growth 30, profitability 30, balance sheet 15, governance and issue structure 25. Grades are quintiles within each segment. "After listing" compares the latest price with the listing-day close, from Chittorgarh's sector tables; it covers only part of the sample and a few weeks of trading.

06

Method and settings

  • The trade. Apply at the upper band. If allotted, sell at the listing price less slippage (mainboard 0.5%, SME 2%), after STT, exchange, GST and DP charges. Short-term gains tax of 20.8% is netted per financial year. Cash is blocked from the bid day until the refund (two working days) or the sale proceeds (listing plus one day).
  • Allotment odds. 1 ÷ retail subscription where known, otherwise total subscription. Application counts are not published, so true odds may be somewhat better.
  • Testing the calls. Each IPO is scored using only history from before its bid date. GMP is the closing-day figure where available, otherwise the listing-eve figure, which is slightly optimistic. Final subscription is known only as bidding closes.
  • Samples. A few hundred IPOs per segment from one market phase. Small groups are marked. This is descriptive analysis, not investment advice.
